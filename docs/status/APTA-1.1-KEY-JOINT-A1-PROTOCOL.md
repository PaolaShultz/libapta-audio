# E2-A1 amplitude-folding candidate — frozen 2026-09-13

N1 numerical repair remains independently versioned and unchanged. The original
E2 is rejected history. This records one bounded attribution/representation
candidate before implementation or fresh waveform generation. No parameter sweep.

## Observed support and limits

N1 scores 130/144 on the spent E2 bank with three breaks, zero numerical failures
and zero high-confidence errors. All true fundamentals survive shortlisting in
the two original valid-solve missing-major errors and the newly exposed missing-
minor error. Known-support diagnostic fits restore these keys, at higher objective:
the unrestricted surrogate rewards extra explanations, not true source recovery.
For all 12 unequal major fixtures, even exact source amplitudes, squared/folded
per window, cause the native selector to choose minor; equal-source diagnostic
chroma restores major. The full instrument happens to get two of those right.
This identifies representation/native-profile sensitivity as a distinct mechanism.
It does not prove that physical harmonic attribution can be repaired by folding.

## Single candidate and hypothesis

A1 uses linear nonnegative physical amplitude mass per pitch class in place of
squared physical amplitudes, then the identical per-window L1 normalization.
Implement as a separate function/instrument. All peak discovery, shortlisting,
model columns, normalization, objective, ridge, N1 kernel, solver budget, numerical
gates, accumulation and native selection/confidence stay fixed. No thresholds,
source-count rules, learned profiles or equal-note oracle enter the candidate.

Hypothesis: direct amplitude folding reduces dominance introduced by squaring
unequal fitted amplitudes and may reduce mode errors. Risk: weaker spurious
explanations gain relative influence, so missing-fundamental attribution may worsen.
This candidate addresses salience representation only, not coherent partial
interference or identifiability. A rejection ends A1 without a nearby exponent,
weight, dictionary or confidence rescue.
